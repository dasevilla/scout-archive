# Marble Madness Bear Adventure

![Marble Madness Bear adventure belt loop](images/marble-madness.jpg)

- **Adventure name:** Marble Madness
- **Rank:** Bear
- **Type:** Elective
- **Category:** 

## Overview

Long before board and video games, kids played with marbles. Those little balls of glass are just as fun to play with now as they were back then. In this Adventure, you’ll play several different marble games. You’ll also learn special words only marble players know and discover how to use marbles in mazes, obstacle courses, and more. Are you ready? Then grab your taws, aggies, and cat’s eyes, and let’s play marbles.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

Make a marble bag.

**Activities:**

- **[Make a Marble Bag](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/make-a-marble-bag/)** (Indoor, energy 2, supplies 3, prep 2)
  Create a personal drawstring bag to hold your marbles.
- **[No Sew Marble Bag](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/no-sew-marble-bag/)** (Indoor, energy 2, supplies 3, prep 2)
  Make a personal drawstring bag to hold your marbles without sewing.

### Requirement 2

Learn five words that are used when talking about marbles.

**Activities:**

- **[Marble Jargon](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/marble-jargon/)** (Indoor, energy 2, supplies 2, prep 2)
  Cub Scouts l earn  marble jargon.

### Requirement 3

Play three different marble games.

**Activities:**

- **[Ringer, Plums, and Dropsies](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/ringer-plums-and-dropsies/)** (Indoor, energy 3, supplies 3, prep 2)
  Learn and  play Ringer, Plums, and Dropsies.

### Requirement 4

Build a marble racetrack with at least two lanes.

**Activities:**

- **[Marble Racetrack](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/marble-racetrack/)** (Indoor, energy 3, supplies 3, prep 5)
  Create a marble racetrack from recycled materials.

### Requirement 5

Build a marble maze.

**Activities:**

- **[Zig, Zag, Zoom](https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/zig-zag-zoom/)** (Indoor, energy 3, supplies 3, prep 3)
  Create a maze for marbles.
