# Fire Safety Merit Badge
![Fire Safety Merit Badge](images/fire-safety-merit-badge.jpg)

## Overview



The ability to use fire safely is essential to human survival. By earning this merit badge, Scouts will learn to uses fire safely and responsibly, how to prevent home fires, and how to handle fire safely, as well as burn prevention, and camping safety.

## Requirements

- (1) **Science of and Hazards of Fire.** Do the following:
  - (a) Explain the concept of fire.

    **Resources:** [Is Fire a Solid, a Liquid, or a Gas? (video)](https://youtu.be/YV8TT9LRBrY), [What Is Fire? (video)](https://youtu.be/tMDKeBaLWDw)
  - (b) Name the parts of the fire tetrahedron and the products of combustion.

    **Resources:** [Understanding the Fire Tetrahedron (video)](https://youtu.be/Bd9JWtp-3wI), [Fire Tetrahedron \| What Are the Elements Required in Order To Ignite Fire (video)](https://youtu.be/c7bFL_-vQQA), [Science Spotlight: The Combustion of Wood (video)](https://youtu.be/B0E4PX3e3RE), [Fire and Flame 09—Hydrocarbon Combustion Products (video)](https://youtu.be/_wzJQFl1k9I)
  - (c) Describe the life cycle of a fire.

    **Resources:** [Fire Safety Stages of Fire (video)](https://youtu.be/nrMlNAPuJM0), [Stages of a Fire (video)](https://youtu.be/IEXKxnSPDY0), [The Stages of Fire (video)](https://youtu.be/AI5BTfeH05A)
  - (d) Explain the three methods of heat transfer.

    **Resources:** [Heat Transfer (video)](https://youtu.be/8UAI5WFC-2g)

- (2) **Fire Extinguishers.** Do the following:
  - (a) Explain the different classes of fires.

    **Resources:** [The 5 Different Classes of Fires (video)](https://youtu.be/FeLFhRcOWtA)
  - (b) Identify the different classes of fire extinguishers and which should be used for each class of fire.

    **Resources:** [Choose the Right Fire Extinguisher (video)](https://youtu.be/P47BltapQi4)
  - (c) Demonstrate how to use a fire extinguisher and a fire blanket.

    **Resources:** [How To Use a Fire Extinguisher (video)](https://youtu.be/s1fne087XAk), [How To Use a Fire Extinguisher Using the PASS Method (video)](https://youtu.be/PQV71INDaqY), [How To Use a Fire Blanket (video)](https://youtu.be/DPsceUJPth8)

- (3) **Fire-related Injuries.** Do the following:
  - (a) Explain and demonstrate the technique of stop, drop, roll, cool, and call.

    **Resources:** [Stop, Drop, Roll, Cool, Call (video)](https://youtu.be/ohntbBVtSKg), [Stop, Drop, and Roll (website)](https://www.nfpa.org/news-blogs-and-articles/blogs/2022/03/31/not-your-mothers-stop-drop-and-roll-evolution-of-a-key-fire-safety-message), [Stop, Drop, Roll, Cover Your Face (video)](https://youtu.be/NzAQR8vHa14), [Stop, Drop, and Roll (video)](https://youtu.be/TjHar3TT4tU)
  - (b) Describe how clothing fires occur, how to prevent them, and how clothing can prevent or contribute to burn injuries.

    **Resources:** [Flame Resistant Fabric Demonstration (video)](https://youtu.be/WFI3M0igb2o)
  - (c) Identify the most frequent causes of burn injuries and how to prevent them.

    **Resources:** [How Common Burns Are, and How They Are Caused (video)](https://youtu.be/RE8sUY0_RTo)
  - (d) Review the prevention of, symptoms and signs of, and treatment of airway burns and carbon monoxide (CO) poisoning.

    **Resources:** [Burn Prevention Tips From Nurses (video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/h7YHWymf7uk), [Signs and Treatment for Carbon Monoxide Poisoning (video)](https://www.tiktok.com/@redcrosscanada/video/7234607871151967493)

- (4) **Origins of Fires.** Do the following:
  - (a) Explain the four classifications of fire origin (natural, accidental, incendiary, or undetermined) and give an example of each.

    **Resources:** [Undetermined Cause: What's That About (website)](https://ocig.com/ocig-institute/undetermined-cause-whats-that-about/)
  - (b) Describe how a fire classified as incendiary might lead to criminal prosecution of a person charged with arson.

    **Resources:** [Criminal Law: The Crime of Arson (video)](https://youtu.be/waDjv5UXVMY)

- (5) **Fire Safety at Home.** Do the following:
  - (a) Describe how to prevent the frequent causes of home fires, including unattended cooking, electricity, smoking, clothes dryers, and rechargeable batteries.

    **Resources:** [5 Most Common Causes of House Fires (website)](https://www.injurylawyers.com/blog/5-leading-causes-of-house-fires/), [Fireworks and Wildfires (video)](https://youtu.be/1WOO-Sn2pNI)
  - (b) Describe how to prevent frequent causes of seasonal and holiday-related home fires, including fires related to home heating, holiday trees, candles, and fireworks.

    **Resources:** [How To Prevent Holiday Fires (video)](https://youtu.be/6ggmmzFSgpU)
  - (c) Demonstrate how to safely light and extinguish a candle. Discuss with your counselor, then with your family, how to safely use candles.

    **Resources:** [How To Properly Extinguish Your Candles (video)](https://www.tiktok.com/@naturasoy/video/7060598766985858309)
  - (d) Using the Home Fire Safety Checklist in the *Fire Safety* merit badge pamphlet or one approved by your counselor, and with the help of an adult, inspect a home (or a similar building near where you live or at a camp) for fire safety hazards. Present your completed checklist to your counselor and discuss your findings.

    **Resources:** [Home Fire Safety Checklists (PDF)](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/Merit_Badge_ReqandRes/Requirement%20Resources/Fire%20Safety/Home%20Fire%20Safety%20Checklists.pdf)
  - (e) Determine if, and what types of, smoke and CO alarms are required in homes in your community. List what types of smoke and CO alarms your home has, their locations, and their expiration dates.

    **Resources:** [Understanding Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Detectors (video)](https://youtu.be/LKmhqdq1YnI)
  - (f) Demonstrate maintenance of smoke and CO alarms to your counselor and review plans to replace expired alarms.

    **Resources:** [Home Smoke Alarm Testing and Maintenance (video)](https://youtu.be/ZMIHE7UeFTU), [Smoke Alarm Maintenance (video)](https://youtu.be/JvjLPSja6xU)
  - (g) Develop a home fire-escape plan and a fire-drill schedule with your family, draw a floor plan of your home with exits marked and a map showing your family meeting point, and conduct a home fire drill.

    **Resources:** [How To Survive a Fire (video)](https://youtu.be/GAiw8PexsOU), [Home Fire Escape Plans (website)](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prepare-for-fire/home-fire-escape-plans/), [Home Fire Escape Plans (PDF)](https://www.nfpa.org/downloadable-resources/pdf/how-to-make-a-home-fire-escape-plan)
  - (h) Explain what to do and what not to do if you smell natural gas and if you smell smoke.

    **Resources:** [Gas Explosion Prevention (video)](https://youtu.be/-Z_4TBcXamI), [What To Do if You Smell Gas (video)](https://youtu.be/RPJGdDOeGx0)
  - (i) Explain how you would report a fire to have the fire department respond.

    **Resources:** [How To Call 911 (video)](https://youtu.be/JdKG_L5YuB8), [What Happens When You Call 911 (video)](https://youtu.be/cmEa7Y0ZF5s)

- (6) **Safety with Flammable and Combustible Liquids.** Do the following:
  - (a) Explain what flammable and combustible liquids are commonly used in homes and how to safely store them.

    **Resources:** [Flammable Liquids (video)](https://youtu.be/ofH8KxvtfZw), [Storing Flammable Liquids Safely (video)](https://youtu.be/CaDK26g9PxA)
  - (b) Describe the safe way to refuel a liquid-fueled engine, such as a lawn mower, outboard motor, farm machine, or automobile with fuel from an approved container.

    **Resources:** [How To Pump Gas (video)](https://youtu.be/Ujsly7RUr7g)
  - (c) Describe the options available for safely disposing of unwanted flammable, combustible, and hazardous substances in your community.

    **Resources:** [How To Dispose of Gasoline (video)](https://youtu.be/JJXoaEOC_iI), [How To Dispose of Old Gasoline (video)](https://youtu.be/elXHfp56jMQ)

- (7) **Fire Safety in Public Buildings.** Do the following:
  - (a) Explain how public buildings are designed for fire safety.

    **Resources:** [Fire Safety in High-Rise Buildings (website)](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/blog/protecting-people-who-live-or-work-in-high-rises/), [Fire Safety in Assembly Occupancies (website)](https://www.nfpa.org/education-and-research/building-and-life-safety/nightclubs-assembly-occupancies)
  - (b) Explain what fire safety systems and equipment can be found in public buildings.

    **Resources:** [How To Conduct a Fire Drill (video)](https://youtu.be/MFmEYVtaqGY)
  - (c) Explain what fire safety precautions you should take when you are in a public building.

    **Resources:** [NFPA Tips for Fire Safety in Public Buildings (website)](https://ohsonline.com/articles/2016/12/13/nfpa-offers-tips-for-fire-safety-in-public-buildings.aspx)

- (8) **Fire Safety at Camp.** Do the following:
  - (a) Explain how the Leave No Trace Seven Principles and the Outdoor Code apply to camping fires.

    **Resources:** [Leave No Trace Principle 5: Minimize Campfire Impacts (video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/knJwXPeiA20)
  - (b) Demonstrate lighting a match safely, the proper way to extinguish it, and to dispose of it. Explain the similarities and differences between safety matches and strike-anywhere matches.

    **Resources:** [How To Light Paper Matches Safely (video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/OinO3OPTWto), [Fire 101 How To Strike Matches (video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/3X_t7bSs_qQ)
  - (c) Explain and demonstrate making sparks with flint and steel or with a ferro rod and catching a spark to create a flame. Extinguish the flame safely using one of principles of the fire tetrahedron.

    **Resources:** [Making Fire With Flint and Steel (video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/EeR8qutMlWc), [How To Start a Fire With Flint and Steel (video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/Xl7VPPFQayE), [How To Start a Campfire in the Rain? (video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/CXsmV34DqXg)
  - (d) Demonstrate the safe way to start a charcoal fire.
  - (e) Describe three common campsite fire lays. Collect, prepare, and set up tinder, kindling, and fuel for a campfire following the Leave No Trace Seven Principles and the Outdoor Code. Ignite your fire lay and safely extinguish your fire.

    **Resources:** [3 Campfire Lays—Through the Eyes of a Thermal Camera (video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/Swgwbk0mcH4)
  - (f) Demonstrate using a camp stove.

    **Resources:** [How To Use a Camping Stove (video)](https://www.tiktok.com/@therealdadhowdoi/video/7131121409199197482)
  - (g) Explain how to set up and keep a campsite safe from fire.

    **Resources:** [Campfire Safety \| How To Make a Fire While Camping \| How To Put Out a Campfire Safely (video)](https://youtu.be/E0YqVXJ4mkc)
  - (h) Develop a lesson plan to teach a fire safety skill or topic, such as lighting a candle or a match or a charcoal fire or a lantern or a camp stove or using flint and steel or setting up a fire lay. Review your lesson plan with your counselor. Teach your skill or topic to one or more Scouts using the EDGE method.

    **Resources:** [The Teaching EDGE: The Best Way To Teach Someone a New Skill (website)](https://blog.scoutingmagazine.org/2017/05/05/living-on-the-edge-this-is-the-correct-way-to-teach-someone-a-skill)

- (9) **Wildland Fires.** Do the following:
  - (a) List causes of wildland fires and ways to prevent them.

    **Resources:** [Wildfires 101 (video)](https://youtu.be/5hghT1W33cY)
  - (b) Explain the social, economic, and environmental consequences of wildland fires.

    **Resources:** [How Are Wildfires Making Us Sick (video)](https://youtu.be/8mQllQnJrhg), [How Wildfires Affect Water Supply (video)](https://youtu.be/RV_OZ430GAs), [A Better Way To Think Abut Wildland Fires (video)](https://youtu.be/HeOl2N6iLy0)

- (10) **Careers.** Do ONE of the following:
  - (a) Visit a fire station in-person. Identify the various types of fire service vehicles. From the firefighters you meet during your visit, learn about the fire prevention activities in your community and about their careers. Discuss with your counselor what you learned during your tour and interviews.

    **Resources:** [Fire Station Tour for Kids (video)](https://youtu.be/aKukWE4CUtU), [Memphis Fire Department Virtual Field Trip (video)](https://youtu.be/qO6j6oJtK_Q)
  - (b) Identify three career opportunities that would use skills and knowledge in the field of fire safety. Pick one and research the training, education, certification requirements, experience, and expenses associated with entering the field. Research the prospects for employment, starting salary, advancement opportunities and career goals associated with this career. Discuss what you learned with your counselor and whether you might be interested in this career.

    **Resources:** [How To Become a Firefighter (video)](https://youtu.be/knS_Dxyu9Rk), [Wildland Firefighting and Wildland Fire Ecology Jobs (video)](https://youtu.be/55iglSp3ki0)
